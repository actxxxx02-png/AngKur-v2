PromptCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

PromptCut is a video editing app. It edits the videos you pick on your own phone. This policy explains what data leaves your phone, where it goes and why.

Data that stays on your phone

Your videos, the finished videos, the app's analysis of your clips (shots, faces detected, labels, loudness) and your settings stay on your phone. PromptCut has no account, no ads, no analytics and runs no servers of its own. We, the developer, never receive your videos or data.

Data sent to third-party services you choose

  • AI planning (Google Gemini or Groq). To plan an edit, the app sends a text description of your clips (durations, shot times, detected objects, number of faces, speech transcripts) and your instructions to the AI service you set up with your own API key. With Google Gemini, small still frames from your clips (about 36 low-resolution images) are also sent. These services process the data under their own terms and privacy policies: Google, Groq.
  • Speech-to-text (Groq), only if you save a Groq key. The audio track of your clips is sent to Groq to turn speech into text, so the app avoids cutting people off mid-sentence.
  • Free music (Openverse). Music search words are sent to Openverse, and songs you choose are downloaded from their hosts (for example Jamendo).

Your API keys

API keys you enter are encrypted on your phone with the Android Keystore and are sent only to the service they belong to.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos (system photo picker): only the videos you pick are accessed.
  • Internet: to reach the services above.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show progress and keep editing while the screen is off.

Children

PromptCut is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Data sent to Google or Groq is handled under their policies; you can delete your API keys in their consoles.
